EXO-CYBER PATCH

Files:
- messageHandler.js: rebuilt handler with private/public gate and anti-link enforcement.
- owner.js: .sudo, .delsudo, .mode, .antilink commands.
- index.js: preserves your existing modules and registers owner.js; uses config.prefix.

Install:
1. Back up your current files.
2. Replace messageHandler.js and index.js with these versions.
3. Add owner.js beside index.js.
4. Keep your uploaded settings.js, config.js, helpers.js, and general.js unchanged.
5. Restart the bot and check Railway logs.

Commands:
- .sudo @user (owner only)
- .delsudo @user (owner only)
- .mode private
- .mode public
- .antilink off
- .antilink delete
- .antilink kick
- .antilink kickanddelete
- Aliases: .antilinkkick and .antilinkdelete

Notes:
- The bot needs group-admin permission to delete other users' messages and remove members.
- Only the current group's own WhatsApp invite link is exempt; other group invite links and external URLs are moderated.
- In private mode, only owner/sudo users can use bot commands or AI.
- View-once media is not cached or extracted by this handler.
